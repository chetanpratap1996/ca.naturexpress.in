# Phase 0: Brains & Taxes workflow definition

## Pilot workflow

**Working hypothesis:** start with one recurring monthly GST engagement for one legal entity and one return period. Confirm whether GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B is the best first workflow with the partner and the staff who prepare it. This document is a discovery baseline, not a statement of the firm's approved process or statutory advice.

## Reference journey

1. A partner or manager opens an engagement for an entity and period, sets the statutory due date from a verified source, selects an owner, and records the documents requested.
2. The client sees the engagement and missing-document list in the portal. They upload a document against that engagement from their phone or computer.
3. The upload appears in the firm's document queue, linked to the entity, period, and engagement. Staff review it and either accept it or ask for a correction with a reason.
4. The person responsible confirms the required document list is complete, then advances the engagement through preparation, review, and partner sign-off.
5. The firm records the filing date and government acknowledgement/reference. The client sees the firm-reported status and reference.
6. The activity trail shows who changed the status, document review result, and filing reference, with timestamps.

## Decisions to confirm before pilot data is loaded

- Which one engagement should be piloted first (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, or another recurring service)?
- Which documents are required for that service, by client type, and who can approve exceptions?
- Who owns preparation, review, and partner sign-off? Can the same person hold more than one role?
- What source is authoritative for each deadline, and who verifies deadline changes?
- Should a client see all entity-level tasks, or only tasks explicitly shared with that client contact?
- How should staff request a correction, and what response time is expected?
- Which acknowledgements count as proof of completion for each service?
- What current tools remain authoritative during the pilot, and what is the process for reconciling them?

## Pilot baseline and success measures

Record these for a representative set of engagements before rollout, then compare weekly:

- Minutes staff spend creating/assigning each engagement.
- Time from first document request to a complete, usable document set.
- Number of follow-ups and correction cycles per engagement.
- Time spent finding the latest file or determining current ownership/status.
- Number of overdue engagements and the reason each is blocked.
- Percentage of pilot engagements completed end-to-end in the product.
- Client completion rate from the secure link, including clients who do not install the PWA.

Agree target improvements with the firm before the pilot. Do not use invented adoption or time-saving claims in sales material.

## Interview prompts

Ask one partner, one manager/preparer, and at least two clients to walk through their last completed engagement. Ask them to show where each request, upload, correction, approval, and acknowledgement lived. Capture actual steps, exceptions, and time spent; do not lead with proposed features.
